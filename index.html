About Kora Studio

At Kora Studio, we believe jewellery should feel effortless, personal, and made to last. We design modern, minimalist pieces created to be lived in—whether you are dressing up for a milestone or adding a subtle touch of shine to your everyday routine. Focusing on clean lines, thoughtful craftsmanship, and enduring quality, our collections are made to be layered, loved, and worn your way.
